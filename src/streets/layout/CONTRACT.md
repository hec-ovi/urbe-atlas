# Street layout

Places a rectangular street grid directly from lane widths and whole sidewalk panel counts.

`GridLayout.plan(input)` takes [GridLayoutInput](schema.ts) and returns [GridLayoutPlan](schema.ts). Street profiles supply 1, 2 or 4 lanes. A caller selects each side's resolved sidewalk profile and finish from its district. Paved widths must be 2, 4 or 6 m with the module kit's 20 cm curb and 30 cm gutter.

`highway: true` reserves one four-lane interior through-run before block dimensions are calculated. The independent highway seed stream chooses its row or column within the middle half of the grid indices. At least one block remains on both sides, including in small cities. `highwayRunId` identifies the reserved grade run; the caller assigns its highway class and elevations. Omission or false publishes no highway reservation.

Row and column lengths vary independently, producing square and rectangular blocks. Each axis includes a shorter and a longer span while preserving its total panel count. Each row and column keeps one road profile. Blocks contain whole 2 m panel groups, fixed corner pieces and their buildable rectangles. Graph intersections are row/column identities. Roadway rectangles have disjoint interiors. Modules supply the road-facing corner returns. No line tracing or polygon intersection is needed to place the base grid.

The reserved rectangle remains inside the requested size. An optional perimeter profile constructs continuous outer sidewalks and supplies their directed edge sections. It requires whole-metre road spans. The caller owns the remaining land outside the roadway and sidewalk rectangle. Native parking occurs on at most one 6 m paved frontage per selected block, with a 15% selection probability and complete clearances. Reserved highway frontages are ineligible. Bays have 6 by 2.5 m slots, 2 m diagonal ends and 2 m support aprons; the complete support stays 6 m clear of corners. Output includes explicit bay and slot footprints and 3.5 m walking clearance. A selected side publishes a derived parking profile with walking interval 3..6.5 m from the road edge, over its existing rear sidewalk. Overall paved/side width and bay geometry remain fixed. Custom profiles requiring more than 3.5 m walking width are ineligible. Guardrails occupy 2, 4 or 6 m groups on sparse seeded frontages, at most two groups per block. Parking and rail choices use independent seed streams. Same seed, settings and side selection produce identical data.

Optional `diagonals` is `candidates` (default), `off`, or `legacy-applied`. Candidate mode publishes `diagonalCandidates`, [schema.ts](schema.ts), without changing any graph, block, module or reservation. Off publishes an empty array. Invalid modes fail; candidate failure never applies a cut.

Candidate sources are the original block rectangles and their ordered south/east/north/west graph edges. Blocks with parking or reserved highway frontages are excluded. The adapter enumerates all face pairs within each eligible rectangle and opposite outer faces across immediate edge-sharing neighbors. Neighbor proposals include both shared-street faces as intermediate mouths. Allowed land comprises the participating original rectangles and authored roadway rectangles. Every full reserved mouth stays `diagonalCornerClearance` metres from original corners (default 3). The [candidate planner](diagonal-candidates/CONTRACT.md) returns individual straight 30/45 degree midpoint proposals with exact full-footprint coverage.

Each proposal identifies its chosen first available one/two-lane street profile and resolved sidewalk sections at the source group's centre. Actual `constructionWidth` is the road plus both complete sidewalk widths. Separate `reservationPadding` is 2.5 m per side for conservative returns; `reservationWidth`, footprint and all mouth checks include it. These records are proposals, not reservations or constructed streets. `LayoutCandidates.retain(candidates, ownerIdMap)` discards proposals whose terminal/intermediate rectangles were removed and maps surviving rectangle IDs; stable candidate IDs and geometry remain unchanged.

Explicit `legacy-applied` reproduces sparse local block cuts: one/two lanes, module junctions at least 40 m from original nodes, nonadjacent blocks and one cut per 20 blocks where feasible. Graph splits preserve source stations and frontage references. This compatibility mode publishes no candidate descriptors.

The plan retains `planning.frontages` and `planning.corners` from the original module producers. Frontages name their actual source graph edges; graph splits update those references. Diagonal templates replace the original block supports with their authored contour supports. Outer sidewalks retain their own directed supports. Null corner references mark straight handoffs between owners. `planning.protected` carries non-owning underpass references supplied by the underpass producer. This metadata assigns no additional ground.

`LayoutPlanning.retain(planning, ownerIdMap)` filters source supports to retained owners and applies the caller's owner-ID mapping. Frontage/corner IDs and all coordinates remain unchanged; references between surviving supports retain their original identity. It changes only this planning metadata.

Invalid profiles or nonboolean feature flags throw `E_INVALID_PARAMS`; a size that cannot fit two blocks on each axis throws `E_UNSATISFIABLE`.
